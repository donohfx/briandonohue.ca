Night Atlas is a nautical-style map of Nova Scotia that moves. Ships report their positions, lighthouses blink in their charted colours, and at night the sweeping beams stop at the shoreline.

It began as the background on my Linux desktop and then moved to the web, so anyone can look around, switch between chart and night, or take the tour into Halifax Harbour.

The fiddly part was getting the lighthouses to feel right. Charts tell you how a light flashes, not whether anything actually turns, so the beams are a best guess. Please don’t navigate by it.

I built it with Claude Code and OpenAI Codex doing much of the coding. The [code and desktop setup are on GitHub](https://github.com/donohfx/night-atlas), with credits for the data behind it.
