I moved into product through tax, first on a secondment and then as the PM responsible for how Shopify calculated, reported, and partnered on sales tax.

That meant owning the in-house calculation engine, the reporting merchants relied on at filing time, and the Avalara partnership for merchants who needed more.

Tax rules are detailed, they change, and when you get them wrong a real business pays for it. That’s where I learned to respect edge cases and explain complicated things plainly.
