year: '2026'

Most AI video tools make you regenerate the whole video every time you change a word. That gets old fast on something like a training video, where most of the work is small fixes.

web-video treats a video as a web page. Slides, charts, captions, a moving cursor, narration: it’s all HTML and code an agent can edit directly, with a live preview in the browser. When it looks right, it renders once to a frame-exact MP4.

I built it for my own training videos. Next step is sharing it more widely.
