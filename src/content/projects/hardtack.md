Hardtack is a 1976 fibreglass sailboat that I race out of Halifax. It’s quick when everything works, which isn’t often.

Most of owning an old boat is fixing it. Rigging, wiring, the engine, plumbing. If something breaks, I want to know why before I replace it.

When I have crew on board, I explain what we’re doing and why instead of just calling it out. It slows things down at first. People get better faster, and it’s more fun when everyone knows what’s going on.

## 2026

This year mostly didn’t happen. The engine started leaking oil early in the season. I replaced the oil drain hose and the banjo bolt, and it’s still leaking, so I haven’t found it yet.

I also had a lot going on off the water and took a step back from racing. The new sail never came out of its bag.

The boat’s on the hard for the winter. In the spring I’ll find the leak and get the new sail up. The rigging and engine projects are still waiting after that.
