Hardtack is a 1976 fibreglass sailboat that I race out of Halifax. It’s quick when everything is working, and something usually isn’t.

Owning an old boat is mostly systems work (rigging, electrical, the engine, the plumbing nobody wants to talk about). Each piece is simple on its own, and they all interact.

On race days I explain why we’re doing things instead of just calling instructions. It takes longer. The crew learns a lot more.

## 2026

This season was a bust. An oil leak showed up early. I replaced the oil drain hose and the banjo bolt, and it’s still leaking.

Between that and a lot going on off the water, I took a step back for the year. The new sail never came out of its bag, which stings.

The boat is on the hard for the winter. Next season starts with finding that leak, then finally putting the new sail up. The rigging refit and engine rebuild are still on the list.
