For most of its life, Shop Pay only worked if your store ran on Shopify. The Shop Pay Commerce Component let merchants on any platform, including fully custom ones, offer it to their buyers.

It took more than two years of iteration and merchant conversations to get there. A lot of the work happened outside product. I joined sales calls, answered merchant questions live, fed what we heard back into positioning, and helped close our first enterprise deals.

Our first merchant was Everlane, which runs its own homegrown commerce platform. We landed them right before Black Friday, and leadership wanted a big NRF announcement in January with almost no prep time. So we changed the plan and announced alongside Everlane, with a case study and a video interview I recorded with their product lead, Anna Peterson.

Within 30 days, 15% of Everlane’s U.S. transactions went through Shop Pay, and conversion peaked at up to 70%.

After launch I set up the adoption roadmap for enterprise merchants, then moved to Shop Pay Installments and on-platform growth when the business needed it. I stayed on Shop Pay and Shop, as product lead from 2024, until I left Shopify in November 2025.
